The Atelier

Search the way you see.

Every piece is read on two independent axes — design, via a self-supervised vision model (DINOv2) that reads pattern and structure in grayscale, and colour, via a perceptual palette compared with optimal transport. A single slider lets you weight one against the other, because “find this design” and “find this colour” are different searches. Type instead of upload, and a vision-language model (CLIP) reads your words.
